# Worlds 1-3: removing repetition

Each later level (x-2 to x-5) gets one hook that runs through it, and each room idea appears once per
world. Good, unique rooms stay as they are; repeats are replaced with new room types built from the
same mechanics. Level 1 of each world (the original five levels) is unchanged.

## Room ideas that appear once each

World 1 (mines, shells, spikes)
- Pit kick + pop over a step (A01)
- Long pit with a mid-air shell catch (A02)
- Spiked slab corridor ending in a mine slot (A04)
- Flat kicks under a low spiked ceiling (B01)
- Eaved shaft climb (B02)
- Golden notch in a spiked ceiling (B04)
- Mine elevator + kick across (B05)
- Two-mine chimney ladder (C01)
- Island drop + golden dip (C03)
- Mine spire + shell kick (C04)
- Two-mine bank shot (D05)
- New: sideways mine blast (shoot a mine beside you to be thrown flat across a gap)
- New: shell ladder (climb a shaft by popping up and catching floating shells)
- New: braking well (fall down a spiked well and shoot down to stop on narrow ledges)
- New: switchback (cross a room left-to-right low, then come back right-to-left high)
- New: one-shell gauntlet (gun capped at one shell by refills placed one at a time)

World 2 (adds spore pods)
- Pod slingshot onto a high ledge (G01), V pull under a spiked block (G03), mine launch + pod over a
  wall (G04), pod relay with a mine (H03), slot pull through a spiked wall (H05), pull up then flat
  second pod under a spiked ceiling (J02), pod canopy with kick breaks (H01), pod zig-zag shaft (H02)
- New: pod corner swing (pull into an alcove, then out around a corner)
- New: downward pods (pull down through a spiked shaft fast and brake at the bottom)
- New: pod into mine (slingshot past a mine and shoot it mid-flight for a second boost)

World 3 (adds phase plates)
- Keep the twelve unique rooms of 3-2 to 3-4. 3-5 gets new rooms instead of copies:
  phase rhythm run (floors swap under you as you run), phase + braking well, gated pod descent,
  mine launch through alternating lids.

## Per level

1-2 Cinder Steps (warm-up combos): A01, A02, sideways mine blast, A04, and a short exit.
1-3 Bellwire Pass (spiked ceilings): B01, B02, low-ceiling mine blast, B04, B05 to the gate.
1-4 Hollow Spire (vertical climb): C01, shell ladder, C03, C04 ending at the gate on top.
1-5 Lantern Crown (the gauntlet): braking well, switchback, one-shell gauntlet, bank shot (D05).

2-2 Sporefall Hollow: G01, G02, G03, G04, gate (pod golden shell).
2-3 Canopy Line: H01, corner swing, H03, H04, H05.
2-4 Comet Crater: mine + pod rim, J02, J03, downward pods, J05 replaced by a new gate climb.
2-5 Starmoss Summit: pod into mine, H02-style zig-zag only here (J04 moves out), corner swing variant,
  and a new finale.

3-2 to 3-4 stay. 3-5 The Great Gearwork: new rooms only (see above).

Exit rooms: no more "hop the spike strip to the gate". The gate sits at the end of each level's last
challenge room, or in a short room with its own small twist.

1-3 Bellwire Pass: B01 flat kicks under spikes, B02 eaved shaft, B04 golden notch, B05 mine elevator, gate on B05.
1-4 Hollow Spire: C01 mine ladder, C02 shell ladder (new), C03 island drop, C04 mine spire, gate on top.

## World 4 (Velvet Glacier: crumbling ice, slick ice, plus mines, pods and phase plates)

4-1 is the original level. Every later room is new and appears once.

4-2 Hoarfrost Steps: X01 crumble hops between pods, X02 thin ice (three floors of separate crumbles; stop over the half with no spikes below), X03 slide gate (one switch opens the gate and drops the bridge; shoot it again behind you before the ice carries you into the pit). Gold above the bridge.
4-3 Rime Gallery: Y01 pod brake (pull across a wide pit onto ice that will not stop you; catch a second pod before the spiked wall), Y02 blast stairs (two crumbling pads, each with a mine beside it; the second throws you back the other way, past the gold), Y03 shield (the mine sits under a copper lid and the gate alcove under a teal one; swap, blast off the mine in the air, swap back).
4-4 Avalanche Shaft: Z01 ice stairs (slick steps you cannot stand on; gold out over the spikes under the top ledge), Z02 collapse lift (crumbling floors with holes; pull diagonally through each hole to a pod), Z03 perch (two shots before the perch breaks: the switch overhead, then the shielded mine).
4-5 Glacier Heart: H01 return trip (cross a crumble bridge to the switch, wait for it to regrow, walk home under the teal ceiling the switch brings down; gold over the bridge), H02 glass pod (swap while falling to open the copper pane, then pull the pod out through the side tunnel), H03 cracked heart (land on a crumbling island; the mine hidden under it throws you to the gate).

## World 5 (Echo Orchard: wind gusts, plus everything before)

5-1 is the original level. Every later room is new and appears once. All gusts share one 2 s rhythm (0.9 s calm, 1.1 s gust) that restarts on room entry, so a gust carrying you into a new room drops you into a calm; room joins are built around that.

5-2 Whisper Grove: O01 thermals (three updraft columns over a spike pit; ride one up, kick across in the calm, fly into the far cliff and ride its updraft to the ledge; gold sheltered in the middle column), O02 crosswinds (fall through a narrow shaft crossed by three gusts toward spiked walls; drop in the calm), O03 airlock lift (updraft from a crumble floor through a copper lid then a teal one; the second switch can only be shot sideways from between the lids).
5-3 Gale Terraces: T01 wind brake (a mine throws you onto ice toward a spiked wall and only the headwind stops you; a low ceiling block keeps the gap above unreachable without stopping first), T02 gale relay (pod chain across a pit that is one updraft, spikes above and below; gold low in the pit), T03 ice terraces (slick terraces down to the gate with a tailwind; the gate ledge has spikes behind it).
5-4 Hollow Trunk: K01 fight the lift (low hall where gusts lift you into ceiling spikes; shoot upward to stay down, never over the pits; gold just under the spikes), K02 draught steps (climb alternating ice and bark ledges, each with a gust toward its spiked wall; bark holds you, ice does not), K03 crown gate (step into the draught as the gust starts; it tops out just under a crosswind that blows onto spikes, and the gate is a kick away in the calm).
5-5 Echo Crown: C01 shelters (one updraft under a spiked ceiling with bare patches; shelter through each gust, kick across in each calm, a spare shell under each patch and the gold under the last), C02 slit (swing into a copper pane, ride the draught up its face to a switch in a slit, open the pane and swing on), C03 last gust (arrive sliding on ice, blast off a mine past the runway's end into the draught up to the gate).

Room doors: a copper door closes behind the player when a pod in the previous room could otherwise be grabbed through the doorway (H02 in 4-5, C02 in 5-5); it is open in the phase the player leaves in and shuts when the next room resets the phase.
